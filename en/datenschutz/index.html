What data is stored?

The app does not send any data on its own – neither to us nor to third parties (since version 0.8; for older versions see “Note on older versions”). There is no user account, no chat function and no advertising. The app contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Ixilotl stores locally on the device:

  • the game progress including learning progress (all tasks with response times and results), the settings and the name entered in the game,
  • the “early user” flag (thank-you for everyone who installs the app by 30 November 2026),
  • a partly completed feedback form (see “Feedback from the app”),
  • an error log (see below).

Learning progress and name never leave the device. We only receive data if you write to us yourself, for example via the app's feedback form – in that case also your email address (see “Feedback from the app”).

Error log (on the device only): if a technical error occurs in the app, it keeps a local note of the last 20 errors at most: time, app version, the technical error message and the affected program locations – no names, no learning progress. The log is not transmitted. You can view and delete it in the feedback form; it is only sent along if you explicitly tick “Attach error log” there (see “Feedback from the app”).

No cloud backup: the app is excluded from Android device backup. The game progress is therefore not backed up via your Google account either, and uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the device.

Note on older versions (up to 0.7.2)

Up to and including version 0.7.2 (test phase until November 2026), the following also applied:

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google): usage statistics and crash reports – but only if a parent had explicitly consented in the app, separately for each service and switched off by default (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 8 GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Pseudonymous information was transmitted: device model, operating system version, approximate location region (derived from the IP address), app version and language, which areas of the app were opened and which missions were played (without answers, results or learning progress), and crash reports. Google could also transfer data to the USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Art. 45 GDPR; additionally standard contractual clauses). Google deletes usage data after 2 months and crash reports after 90 days.
  • Depending on the version, the game progress could be stored encrypted in the Android backup of your Google account (if device backup was switched on), and an “early user” flag (without name or learning data) in Google Block Store.

With the update to version 0.8, all transmission stops. The stored consent is deleted on the device, and the app removes the Block Store entry the next time it starts. You can delete an older device backup in your Google account (Google Drive → Backups). Firebase data already transmitted is linked to a random device identifier, not to you personally; it is deleted automatically after the periods stated above.

Retention period

Local data remains on the device until the app is uninstalled or the game progress is reset. Exception: the “early user” flag is kept when the game progress is reset and is only deleted when the app is uninstalled. The error log holds the last 20 errors at most; older entries are overwritten automatically, and you can delete it at any time in the feedback form. For the retention of emails, see the sections on email and feedback below.

Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data vis-à-vis the data controller:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR), where it is based on legitimate interest
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Since learning progress is stored exclusively locally on the device, these rights in practice mainly concern emails you have sent us (for example via the feedback form) and the email address they contain. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) — typically the authority in your country or state of residence. For Hesse (Germany), this is the Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

Requesting data deletion

Ixilotl has no user account and does not send any data. Everything the app stores is kept only on your device. This is how to delete it:

  • All app data: uninstall Ixilotl (or clear its data in the Android settings under Apps → Ixilotl → Storage).
  • Game progress only: Settings (gear icon) → “Delete all progress”.
  • Error log only: open the feedback form → “Show” next to the error log → “Delete log”.

Emails to us (e.g. feedback including your email address) are deleted on informal request to support@ixilotl.de or by replying to our email – please write from the same address so that we can match it. We process such requests within 30 days.

For data from older versions (Firebase, up to 0.7.2), see “Note on older versions”: it is only linked to a random device identifier, so we cannot attribute it to a person (Art. 11 GDPR); Google deletes it automatically after 2 months or 90 days respectively.

Children

Ixilotl is designed for primary school children. The app does not send any data; there is no chat function, no social networking and no advertising. The name entered in the game and the learning progress remain exclusively on the device. Children may help fill in the feedback form, but only an adult can send it via their own email program (see “Feedback from the app”). Please do not send us any details about your child.

In-app purchases

The app currently offers no purchases. A full version is planned as a one-time purchase through the Google Play Store. Payment processing and the associated data handling will then be subject to Google's privacy policy; we do not receive any payment data.

This website

The website ixilotl.de is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). When pages are accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address, date and time of access, the page requested and browser information, in order to deliver the website and protect it against abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub privacy statement.

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and embeds no third-party content. Fonts are loaded directly from ixilotl.de, not from external servers.

Feedback from the app and tester thank-you

The app contains a feedback form. It does not send anything itself: when you tap send, your own email program opens with a pre-filled email to feedback@ixilotl.de. Only if you send that email do we receive:

  • your email address (and your name, if your email program includes it),
  • your answers in the feedback form (category, stars, scales, free text),
  • the pre-filled technical details: device manufacturer and model, Android version and app version. No device identifiers are included.
  • only if you tick “Attach error log” (off by default): the error log as readable text – times, app version, technical error messages and program locations, no names and no learning progress. You can view it in the app beforehand.

We receive your email address because the email is sent from your own account. We use it only to answer follow-up questions and to send you the tester thank-you. You can review and change the email before sending it. Before the email program opens, there is a short check for adults (entering a date of birth, which is not stored); children may fill in the form, but only an adult can send it.

Draft stays on the device: a partly completed feedback form is saved locally on the device so that it is still there next time. It is not transmitted, and it is deleted as soon as the email program has opened, when you discard it, when the game progress is reset, or when the app is uninstalled.

  • Purpose and legal basis: we evaluate your feedback to improve the app, answer follow-up questions and send you the announced tester thank-you (a Google Play promo code, voluntary and with no legal entitlement) after the full version goes on sale. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in evaluating your feedback, replying to you and sending you the thank-you you expect when you send it. We will not send you advertising or a newsletter.
  • Recipients: the inbox feedback@ixilotl.de is hosted by our email provider INWX in Germany (see “Our email inboxes”). We do not pass your data on to third parties. We generate the promo code in the Google Play Console; we do not share your email address with Google for this.
  • Retention: we delete your email address and the email once the code has been sent, at the latest three months after the full version goes on sale. For feedback that is not eligible for the thank-you (e.g. after 30 November 2026), we delete them once any follow-up questions have been settled. We keep the content of your feedback and any attached error log only without your name and email address.
  • Please do not send your child's data: please do not send us names, photos or other details about your child. If we receive such details anyway, we delete them.
  • Early deletion: an informal email is sufficient. We will then, however, no longer be able to send you the code.
