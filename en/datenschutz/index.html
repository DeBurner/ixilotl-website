Last updated: 26 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the app Ixilotl and the website ixilotl.de. It is addressed to parents and legal guardians who use Ixilotl for their child. Below we describe each process separately – with its purpose, legal basis, recipients and retention period.

Overview

Ixilotl works without a user account, without advertising and without tracking. Game progress, learning progress and the name entered in the game are stored exclusively on the device; we do not receive them. We only receive data if you contact us yourself, send feedback or buy the full version.

1. Using the app (on the device only)

  • Data: saved game with learning progress (tasks with response times and results), settings, the name entered in the game, the “early user” flag (a thank-you for everyone who installs the app by 30.11.2026), a started feedback form and an error log (the last up to 20 technical errors: time, app version, error message, program location – no names, no learning results).
  • Purpose: the app works, remembers progress and detects technical errors.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app); for the error log Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (stability of the app).
  • Recipients: none. The data does not leave the device. The error log is not transmitted; it is only sent along if you explicitly tick “Attach error log” in the feedback form (see Feedback).
  • Retention: until you uninstall the app or reset the game. The “early user” flag survives a reset and is only deleted on uninstall. The error log holds at most 20 entries; older ones are overwritten, and you can delete it in the feedback form at any time.
  • No cloud backup: the app is excluded from the Android device backup. Game progress is not backed up via your Google account.

2. Free play time and buying the full version

Ixilotl can be played free of charge for 3 hours. The app counts the play time used on the device only; we do not receive it. After that, the full version can be bought from 1 December 2026 as a one-time purchase through Google Play.

Google handles the purchase and payment. Google is responsible for this; Google’s privacy notice applies. We do not receive payment data such as card or account numbers.

  • Data we receive: through the Google Play Console we receive order data for each purchase, for example the Google Play order number, date, product, price and country.
  • Data on the device: the play time used and the unlock status. At start, the app asks Google Play whether a purchase exists for your Google account (“Restore purchase”). This request goes to Google, not to us.
  • Purpose: selling and unlocking the full version, handling withdrawal, refunds and defects, bookkeeping.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract); for keeping records, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (retention duties under commercial and tax law).
  • Recipients: Google as operator of Google Play; otherwise nobody.
  • Retention: play time and unlock status stay on the device until the app is uninstalled. We keep order data for as long as commercial and tax law requires (up to 10 years) and then delete it.

Withdrawal: if you declare your withdrawal by email or contact form, we process your message, your email address and the order number to arrange the refund through Google Play (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR; recipients and retention as under section 3 and above). If you withdraw directly through Google Play, we only learn about the refund.

3. Contacting us by email or contact form

  • Data: your email address, possibly your name, and your message. With the contact form, also technically necessary connection data such as your IP address.
  • Purpose: answering your enquiry.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry concerns the app or a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Recipients: our email provider INWX GmbH, Prinzessinnenstr. 30, 10969 Berlin, Germany (processor, Art. 28 GDPR). The contact form is not on ixilotl.de but with Tally (Tally BV, Ghent, Belgium; processor, Art. 28 GDPR). We do not pass your details on to any other third parties.
  • Retention: we delete enquiries (including the submission at Tally) once they are resolved and no retention obligations apply.

4. Feedback from the app and tester thank-you

The app has a feedback form. It does not send anything itself: when you submit it, your own email program opens with a pre-filled email to feedback@ixilotl.de. We only receive data once you send that email. Children may help fill in the form; only an adult can send it: before the email program opens there is a short check for adults (entering a date of birth, which is not stored), and the form requires explicit consent (a checkbox, not ticked by default). As proof, the email contains a sentence stating that consent was given.

  • Data: your email address (and possibly your name, if your email program sends it); your answers in the form (category, stars, scales, free text); device manufacturer and model, Android version and app version (device identifiers are not sent); only if you tick “Attach error log” (off by default) the error log as readable text. You can see and edit the email before sending.
  • Purpose: we evaluate your feedback to improve the app, answer follow-up questions and send you the tester thank-you (a Google Play promo code; voluntary and without legal entitlement) after the full version goes on sale. We send no advertising or newsletters.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, informally and with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR), e.g. by email to support@ixilotl.de. The lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal is not affected. After a withdrawal we delete your email address; we can then no longer send you the code.
  • Recipients: the mailbox feedback@ixilotl.de is hosted by our email provider INWX (see Contacting us). We generate the promo code in the Google Play Console; we do not pass your email address to Google for this.
  • Retention: we delete your email address and the email once the code has been sent, at the latest three months after the full version goes on sale. For feedback without a thank-you (e.g. after 30 November 2026) we delete them once follow-up questions are resolved. The content of the feedback and any attached error log is kept only without name and email address.
  • Draft on the device only: a started form is saved locally so it is still there next time. It is not transmitted and is deleted once the email program has been opened, when you discard it, when you reset the game, or on uninstall.
  • Please send no data about your child: do not send us names, photos or other details about your child. If we receive such details anyway, we delete them.

5. Closed test phase (until November 2026)

For the closed test, interested people could request access by email via a beta page. For this we entered the Play Store email address in the tester list of the Google Play Console (so Google is also a recipient; legal basis Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The beta page no longer exists. We keep the addresses to send all testers of the closed test a Google Play code for the full version after sales start, as a voluntary thank-you (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; there is no legal entitlement to the code). We then delete them, at the latest three months after sales start – or earlier on informal request; we can then no longer send you the code.

6. This website

  • Data: the website ixilotl.de is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). When pages are accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data: IP address, date and time, requested page, browser details.
  • Purpose: delivering the website and protecting against misuse.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (a secure and functioning website).
  • Recipient / third country: GitHub, USA. The transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), under which GitHub is certified.
  • Retention: according to GitHub’s policies.

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and embeds no third-party content. Fonts are loaded directly from ixilotl.de. The forwarding pages sumpfsoftware.de and sumpfsoftwarestudio.de are also hosted on GitHub Pages; the same applies.

Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the following rights against the controller:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)
  • Lodging a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) – in particular the authority of your country of residence. For Hesse this is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

How to exercise your rights: write to us informally at support@ixilotl.de (please use the address you previously wrote to us from, so we can match your data). We reply within one month (Art. 12(3) GDPR). We also give you the information if we hold no data about you.

Data on the device you delete yourself: uninstall Ixilotl (or, in the Android settings under Apps → Ixilotl → Storage, clear the data); only the game progress via Settings → “Delete all progress”; only the error log in the feedback form under “View” → “Delete log”.
