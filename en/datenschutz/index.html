What data is stored?

Ixilotl stores the game state locally on the device: learning progress (every task with answer times and results), settings, and the name entered in the game. Learning progress and the name never leave the device and are not shared with third parties. Some settings are transmitted as usage data – only with consent (see below).

The app additionally uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (see below). There is no user account and no chat feature. The app shows no advertising – neither personalized nor of any other kind.

Crash and usage data (Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics)

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to detect crashes and understand how the app is used, so we can improve it. This may transmit the following information:

  • Device model, operating system version, coarse location region (country or region, derived from IP address, not precise)
  • Timing and stack traces of crashes
  • App usage events: which areas of the app are opened, and the type and duration of missions played and whether they were finished or cancelled – without answers, results, or learning progress, without real names or contact details
  • App version, app language, and whether read-aloud is switched on

This data is not linked to a user account (there is none) and contains no information that allows direct identification of a person. There is no personalized advertising and the device's advertising ID is not used for this purpose.

This data is only collected if a parent has given consent beforehand, separately for each of the two services (see "Consent by parents"). Without consent, Firebase is not started in the app at all.
More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Consent by parents and legal basis

On first launch (or once after the update) the app asks in the "For parents" area whether the two data shares are allowed. The child is asked to hand the device to a parent; before the question there is a quick check for adults (entering the year of birth, which is not stored). Parents can allow usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) separately; both are off by default. "Allow none" or "Later" keeps everything off – the app then works without any restriction.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) of the German TDDDG for access to the device). As the app is aimed at children, consent is given by a parent (Art. 8 GDPR). The decision, its time and the version of the consent text are stored locally on the device only.

Withdrawal: you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future under Settings (gear icon) → Game → "Privacy (for parents)". Turning off requires no check; transmission stops immediately. The lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal remains unaffected (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

International data transfers

Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics are provided by Google LLC and/or Google Ireland Limited. Data may be transferred to the USA or other third countries as part of this. For the USA, an adequacy decision of the EU Commission exists (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Art. 45 GDPR); Google LLC is certified under this framework. In addition, Google relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Details: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Retention period

Local game data remains on the device until the app is uninstalled or progress is reset.

  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics): device- and event-level data is deleted after 2 months. Aggregated statistics that do not relate to an individual device (e.g. "how many users have version X") may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): Google keeps crash reports and the associated identifiers for 90 days and deletes them afterwards.

For Hesse (Germany), this is the Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

Requesting data deletion

Ixilotl has no user account and does not collect names, email addresses, or any other identifiers that would identify a person. The only data transmitted to Google (crash and usage data via Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics, see above) is linked to a random, pseudonymous device identifier — not to you personally. This means we cannot match a deletion request received by email to a specific person's data (Art. 11 GDPR).

Deletion therefore works directly on the device:

  1. Open Ixilotl
  2. Open Settings (gear icon) → Game
  3. Under "Privacy (for parents)", turn off Usage statistics and Crash reports

This immediately stops transmission and resets the app's device identifier in Firebase Analytics — severing the link between your device and all previously collected pseudonymous data. Local learning progress is unaffected (it is stored exclusively on the device and never sent to us); it can be removed separately by uninstalling the app.

Crash reports not yet sent are deleted on the device without consent. There is no on-device reset for crash reports already sent; these are subject to automatic deletion after 90 days (see "Retention period" above). For anything beyond this — for example if you can provide additional information to identify your specific data, or for general questions about deletion — contact us at ixilotl.app@gmail.com. We handle such requests within 30 days.

Children

Ixilotl is aimed at primary-school-age children. The app does not collect personal data that would allow identification of a child. There is no chat feature, no social networks, and no advertising. The name entered in the game and the learning progress stay on the device only. Usage and crash data are only transmitted if a parent has consented (see "Consent by parents"); consent can be withdrawn at any time in the settings.

Contact by email and beta requests

If you email us or create a request via the beta page (the form merely opens your own email program; the website itself stores nothing), we process the information in your email: your email address, your name if provided, and your message.

  • Purpose: answering your request; for beta requests, activating you as a tester. To do so we add your Play Store email address to the tester list in the Google Play Console.

  • Retention: general requests are deleted once they have been dealt with and no retention obligations apply. Tester addresses are deleted when the test phase ends, or earlier if you no longer wish to take part — an informal email is sufficient.
